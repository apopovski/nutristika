🌿 ABOUT PAGE
Opening
I’m a registered dietitian nutritionist passionate about helping adults improve their health through sustainable, realistic lifestyle habits. My work focuses on anti-inflammatory, whole-food nutrition and behavior change strategies that fit into real life.

My Philosophy
I believe in inclusion rather than restriction. When you consistently add nourishing foods and supportive routines, unhealthy patterns begin to fade naturally.
Health is not about perfection — it’s about building habits that align with your long-term wellbeing.

How I Support You
Educate Evidence-based lifestyle medicine strategies that make sense.
Support Compassionate accountability and personalized guidance.
Empower Tools to overcome roadblocks and build lasting confidence.

My Story
My passion for nutrition began during my freshman year of college when I experienced firsthand how poor habits affected my energy and performance. Learning how daily food choices influence long-term health transformed my life and sparked a calling to help others experience the same empowerment.
Having lived, studied, and worked across Europe, the United States, and Australia, I bring a global appreciation for food culture and whole-food traditions into my practice.

A Few Facts About Me
